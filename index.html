What this is

Video Nuggets is a public, in-browser prototype that replicates the concept of a larger doc-to-video platform I built: it turns plain documents into short, narrated, branded “nugget” lessons, then lets you ask questions about them. The original is a Python/FastAPI service with a heavyweight render pipeline (Ollama, Edge TTS, ffmpeg, LibreOffice, ChromaDB). This replica runs the same stages with zero-cost browser equivalents so anyone can try it instantly — nothing installs, nothing leaves your device.
